Refrigerator Repair & Service

From weak cooling and water leakage to excess frost or a fridge that will not start, start with a clear description of the symptoms. The service team can discuss the appropriate inspection and next steps.

Service overview

When should you request service?

If your refrigerator is not maintaining temperature, has stopped starting, leaks water or builds up unusual frost, describe the symptoms before arranging a visit. The team can explain the likely next step after understanding the appliance and issue.

Common problems we can discuss

  • Fridge not cooling
  • Fridge not starting
  • Water leakage
  • Excessive ice or frost
  • Unusual noise
  • Temperature fluctuation

What may be checked?

Depending on the appliance and symptoms, the technician may need to inspect the power supply, controls, temperature performance, seals, water lines or other relevant components. The exact checks depend on the model and diagnosis.

How the service process works

STEP 01Describe the issue

Tell us the appliance brand, model and symptoms.

Before you call

Note when the issue started, any unusual noise or leak, and any error code. Keep the brand and model number handy if possible. For safety, do not open electrical components or attempt refrigerant repairs yourself.

An enquiry does not confirm an appointment or diagnosis. Timing, parts availability, final charges and supported models must be confirmed with the service provider.
